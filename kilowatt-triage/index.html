01 The question

The hard part isn’t finding upgrades. It’s deciding which one goes first.

Not every school can receive every energy-efficiency upgrade at once. Budgets are limited. Climate conditions differ. Existing heating systems differ. And interventions can affect one another. Kilowatt Triage asks which measures should be prioritized, and why.

    04 The limits

    What we know.
    What we don’t.

    We would rather be precise than impressive. Here is exactly where the evidence stops.

    Known Published

    1. The World Bank models a reference public school: 4,000 m², two floors above ground and a basement.
    2. For that model school it publishes heating intensity, saving potential and investment estimates (Tables 36, 42, 47).
    3. Insulation and boiler measures interact. The report evaluates boilers after insulation in the relevant scenario.
    4. LED lighting saves electricity; insulation and boilers mainly affect thermal energy.

    Not yet Not yet collected

    1. School-specific energy bills and operational data. We have not collected any.
    2. On-site validation and local feedback. Both remain important next steps.
    3. Net effects of fuel switching. Electric boilers and heat pumps add electricity demand, so net energy, cost and environmental benefits need further analysis.
    4. A carbon figure. We don’t have a verified Uzbekistan grid-emissions factor for this analysis, so we publish no emissions reduction.
    5. Current prices. The costs are from the 2021 report, not market quotations.
    6. Coverage. One reference school does not represent every school in Uzbekistan.

    How to read the labels on this page

    Published
    A figure taken directly from the World Bank report.
    Our arithmetic
    A simple calculation we made from published figures, such as a ratio or a total.
    Assumption
    A choice made to keep the work going, such as using the reference school as a starting point. It is not a finding.
    Not yet collected
    Information we don’t have yet and are not pretending to.
