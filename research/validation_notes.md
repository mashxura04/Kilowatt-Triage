# Validation Note: World Bank Boiler Savings

## Objective
To investigate whether the World Bank's reported boiler-retrofit savings percentages are calculated using heating demand remaining after insulation.

## Method
For each climate zone, remaining heating demand was calculated by subtracting the reported insulation savings from baseline heating demand.

The reported gas and efficient coal boiler savings were then divided by this remaining heating demand.

## Results

| Climate zone | Gas boiler saving | Coal boiler saving |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 32.29% | 37.50% |
| 2 | 32.29% | 37.50% |
| 3 | 32.29% | 37.50% |
| 4 | 32.29% | 37.50% |

These percentages match the World Bank's reported values in Table 41.

## Interpretation
The calculations provide strong arithmetic evidence that boiler-retrofit savings are based on heating demand remaining after insulation.

However, this does not independently establish the original calculation sequence. The World Bank's underlying methodology or calculation workbook would be needed for definitive confirmation.

## Limitation
The analysis uses a modelled standard-school baseline and published estimates, not measurements from an individual school in Uzbekistan.

## Conclusion
The results support treating the insulation-first interpretation as a working hypothesis. Combined retrofit savings should not be calculated by simply adding individual intervention estimates.
